Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Who we are

Bentley Studios ("we", "us") is an independent software studio based in Gqeberha, South Africa, run by Duncan Bentley. We build apps for business platforms such as Atlassian and Odoo. This policy explains what personal information we handle, why, and the choices you have. You can reach us about anything in it at duncanmarcbentley@gmail.com.

This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts, and it has no forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, to keep the service running and secure. GitHub handles that information under its own privacy statement. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and animation scripts from cdn.jsdelivr.net and unpkg.com; these services receive your IP address to deliver those files.

Our apps

Each of our apps states on its store listing (such as the Atlassian Marketplace or the Odoo Apps Store) exactly which data it can access and why. Our general rules for every app are:

  • We request only the permissions an app needs to work.
  • We use data only to provide the app's features to you.
  • We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.
  • We do not share personal information with third parties, except service providers we need to run an app, and only under terms that protect it.
  • We keep data only as long as it is needed to provide the service, and delete it when you uninstall the app or ask us to.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your name, email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply and to help you, and we do not add you to any mailing list without your permission.

How we protect information

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No system is perfectly secure, but we take security seriously and act quickly on any issue. If we ever become aware of a breach affecting your information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

Your rights

Under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), and where they apply, the EU and UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to how we process it; and
  • complain to a data protection authority.

To use any of these rights, email us. In South Africa you can complain to the Information Regulator.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted on our app listings.
