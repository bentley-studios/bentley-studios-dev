01 What we believe

We would rather ship one app that does its job perfectly than ten that almost work. Every app we make asks for the least it needs, explains what it touches, and is supported by the person who wrote it.

02 Security and privacy

Built in two halves. Locked as one.

A / Least privilege

Each app requests the narrowest access that lets it do its job, and nothing more.

B / Your data stays yours

We never sell your data or use it for advertising. Every app documents what it reads, what it stores and where.

C / Locked tight

Security reports go straight to the top of the list, and we act on them as a priority.

03 Principles

Four rules for every app we ship.

  1. 3.1

    Security first

    Designed in from the first line of code, not bolted on before launch.

  2. 3.2

    Privacy by design

    We collect as little as possible, keep it only as long as needed, and delete it when you uninstall.

  3. 3.3

    Clean, fast design

    Apps that feel at home in your Atlassian tools and stay out of your way.

  4. 3.4

    Real support

    Questions go to the people who built the app, not a ticket queue.

04 Method

How an app gets made.

  1. 1

    Start with a real problem

    Every app begins with a workflow that wastes a team's time, described by the people who live with it.

  2. 2

    Decide the access first

    Before any code, we list the exact permissions the app needs and cut everything else.

  3. 3

    Build and test

    Small releases, tested against real projects, with performance checked on large instances.

  4. 4

    Publish and support

    Listed on the Atlassian Marketplace with clear docs, then looked after by the person who built it.

06 Questions

What admins ask first.

Can I install one of your apps today?

Not yet. Our first apps are in development, and they will appear here and on the Atlassian Marketplace when they launch.

What data will your apps access?

Only what each app needs to work. Every listing will state exactly which permissions the app requests and why, and our privacy policy sets out the rules every app follows.

Do you sell or share data?

No. We never sell personal information or use it for advertising, and we only share it with service providers we need to run an app, under terms that protect it.

How do I report a security issue?

Email us with "SECURITY" at the start of the subject line and we will treat it as a priority. Our support page has the details.

Let's talk.

Questions about our apps, a partnership or a security report? A person will reply. We're in Gqeberha, South Africa, and work with teams in every time zone.
