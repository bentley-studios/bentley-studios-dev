How we build for Odoo

  • Clean installs. Modules follow Odoo's own conventions, so they install, upgrade and uninstall cleanly.
  • Least privilege. Access rights are set to the minimum each feature needs.
  • Your data stays yours. Modules run inside your Odoo database, and we never sell or share your data.

Buying, installing and support

Modules are bought and downloaded through the Odoo Apps Store, under its terms and refund policy. Support comes from the people who built the module: see our support page, our service level agreement and our privacy policy.

// Questions

Before you install.

Which Odoo versions do you support?

Every module is published for Odoo 15.0, 16.0, 17.0, 18.0, 19.0 and 20.0, and each release runs its automated tests on all six before it goes out.

Community or Enterprise?

Both. The modules only depend on parts of Odoo that both editions share, and they run on Odoo.sh and on your own server. Odoo Online does not accept third-party modules, so they cannot be installed there.

What happens when I move to a new Odoo version?

Download the build for your new version from the Odoo Apps Store and install it in your upgraded database. If you are migrating a database between major versions with one of our modules installed, contact us first and we will help you plan it.

How do refunds work?

Every purchase, licence and refund goes through the Odoo Apps Store under its own terms. Separately, if a feature described on a module's store page does not work, we fix it free of charge.

How do I get support?

Use the Support button on your Odoo Apps Store order, or see our support page. You reach the people who wrote the module.
