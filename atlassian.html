What we're building

Focused apps that take the busywork out of everyday work in Jira and Confluence. Our first apps are in development, and they will be listed here and on the Atlassian Marketplace as soon as they launch.

Got a workflow that wastes your team hours every week? Tell us about it. The best apps start that way.

How we build for Atlassian

  • Least privilege. Each app requests only the Atlassian scopes it needs, and the listing explains every one.
  • Your data stays yours. We never sell it or use it for advertising, and every app documents what it reads and stores.
  • Native feel. Apps that look and behave like part of Jira and Confluence, not a bolt-on.
  • Security first. Vulnerabilities are fixed in line with Atlassian's Marketplace security requirements for partners.

Buying, installing and support

Every app is sold and installed through the Atlassian Marketplace, so billing, licences and installs work the way your admins already know. Support comes from the people who built the app: see our support page, our service level agreement and our privacy policy.
