Support

Every message is read and answered by the person who built the app.

Get help with an app

Email duncanmarcbentley@gmail.com. To help us solve it quickly, please include:

  • the name of the app and the Atlassian product you use it in (for example Jira or Confluence);
  • what you expected to happen, and what happened instead;
  • the steps to reproduce it, plus a screenshot if you can.

Please never send passwords, API tokens or other secrets by email. We will never ask you for them.

When we're available

We are based in South Africa and work Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 SAST (GMT+2). We aim to reply to every message within two business days, and urgent issues that stop an app working come first.

Our full response targets by priority are in our service level agreement.

Report a security issue

If you think you have found a security vulnerability in one of our apps, email duncanmarcbentley@gmail.com with "SECURITY" at the start of the subject line. Please give us a reasonable chance to fix it before sharing it publicly. We treat these reports as our top priority and will keep you updated on progress.

Feature requests

Ideas are welcome. Tell us what you are trying to do and what is getting in the way. The best features come from real workflows.

Privacy questions

See our privacy policy, or email us with "Privacy" in the subject line.
