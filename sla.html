Service level agreement

Last updated 29 October 2026

What this covers

This agreement sets out the support Bentley Studios provides for its apps on every platform we sell through, including the Atlassian Marketplace and the Odoo Apps Store: when we are available, how quickly we respond, and how we handle security issues. It applies to every customer with an active installation of one of our apps, on free and paid plans alike.

How to reach us

Email duncanmarcbentley@gmail.com. Put the app name in the subject line, and start the subject with "SECURITY" for anything security related. Our support page lists what to include so we can help quickly.

Support hours

Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 South African Standard Time (SAST, GMT+2), excluding South African public holidays. Business days in this agreement mean those days.

Response targets

We classify each request when it arrives and aim to send a first response from a person, not an auto-reply, within these times:

PriorityWhat it meansFirst responseUpdates
CriticalA security vulnerability, or the app is unusable for all users1 business dayEvery business day until resolved
HighA core feature is broken and there is no workaround2 business daysEvery 2 business days
NormalA bug with a workaround, or a question about using the app2 business daysWhen there is progress
LowFeature requests and feedback5 business daysWhen the request is planned or declined

These are response targets, not resolution times. Some problems take longer to fix, and we will tell you honestly where things stand and what happens next.

Security issues

Security reports are always handled as Critical. We investigate every report, keep the reporter updated, and fix confirmed vulnerabilities in line with each platform's security requirements for partners, including Atlassian's Marketplace security bug fix policy. Please give us a reasonable chance to fix an issue before sharing it publicly. If a vulnerability affects your data, we will notify you as the law requires; see our privacy policy.

Availability

We do not currently offer an uptime guarantee. Our apps run on the platforms you buy them for, so they also depend on those platforms' own services, and outages on their side are outside our control. Atlassian publishes its status at status.atlassian.com; Odoo modules run inside your own Odoo instance.

What is not covered

  • Problems caused by outages or changes on the host platform (such as Atlassian or Odoo), or by third-party apps and services.
  • Problems caused by customer modifications, unsupported configurations or misuse of the app.
  • Custom development, consulting or training beyond normal use of the app.

Remedies

This agreement describes our support commitments. It does not provide service credits, refunds or other financial remedies. Paid apps are also covered by the terms of the store you bought them from, including its refund policy.

Changes to this agreement

If we change this agreement, we will update the date at the top of this page. We will not reduce our response targets for customers in the middle of a paid subscription term.
